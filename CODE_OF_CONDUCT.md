# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

Be decent to people. Criticise work, not persons. Assume the other party is
acting in good faith until you have real evidence otherwise.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
language, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of
discussion.

## A note specific to this project

Technical disagreement is welcome and expected — a lot of this repository consists
of findings that contradicted its own earlier design, and that only happened
because measurements were allowed to win arguments. Saying "this number is wrong,
here is my measurement" is exactly the contribution the project wants.

What is not welcome is dismissing someone's work or hardware as not worth
measuring. Every conclusion here came from one laptop; results from a Raspberry
Pi, a spinning disk or a decade-old ThinkPad are as legitimate as results from a
server.

## Reporting

Report concerns to the maintainers by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Quilzo/potatomaxx/security/advisories/new)
(which is private to maintainers and works for conduct reports too), or by
contacting a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile.

Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers who cannot act impartially on a
report will recuse themselves.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone whose
behaviour they judge inappropriate, and will state the reason. Enforcement
decisions can be appealed by contacting a maintainer not involved in the original
decision.
